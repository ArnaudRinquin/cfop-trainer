Like 2-look OLL, this is a workhorse: 6 algorithms covering all 21 PLL cases. Permute corners first (2 algs), then edges (4 algs).

### Corner permutation

Look at the top layer corners. If two adjacent corners share their non-yellow sticker color ("headlights"), hold the headlights at the back and do an A-perm. If no side has headlights, do an E-perm — that happens in about 1 solve out of 6.

### Edge permutation

Once corners are solved, you have 4 possible edge states: U-perm (clockwise), U-perm (counter), H-perm (opposite swap), or Z-perm (adjacent swap).
